About HealthCare Plus

Leading the way in healthcare excellence for over 35 years

Our History

Founded in 1985, HealthCare Plus Hospital began with a vision to provide world-class healthcare services to our community. What started as a small 50-bed facility has grown into a 500-bed multi-specialty hospital serving over 100,000 patients annually.

Our commitment to medical excellence, cutting-edge technology, and compassionate care has made us the healthcare provider of choice in the region. We continue to invest in our facilities, technology, and most importantly, our people.

Our Departments

Comprehensive medical specialties under one roof

Cardiology

Comprehensive heart care with advanced diagnostic and treatment options for all cardiovascular conditions including heart attacks, arrhythmias, and heart failure.

Neurology

Expert care for brain and nervous system disorders using cutting-edge technology including stroke care, epilepsy treatment, and movement disorder management.

Orthopedics

Specialized treatment for bone, joint, and musculoskeletal conditions with comprehensive rehabilitation services, joint replacements, and sports medicine.

Pediatrics

Dedicated care for infants, children, and adolescents with a child-friendly environment staffed by pediatric specialists and nurses trained in child care.

Ophthalmology

Complete eye care services including LASIK, cataract surgery, glaucoma treatment, retinal care, and vision correction with the latest technology.

Pathology & Laboratory

State-of-the-art laboratory services for accurate diagnosis and disease detection with 24/7 availability and rapid turnaround times.
